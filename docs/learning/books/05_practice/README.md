## Chapter Summaries

### Chapter 1: Monitoring Heart Health
**From Wearables to Life-Saving Alerts**

Meet Sarah, a 62-year-old teacher whose smartwatch detected an irregular heartbeat. This chapter walks through cardiac monitoring workflows, from consumer devices to clinical analysis. You'll learn how arrhythmia detection works, what AFib looks like in data, and the step-by-step process of catching heart problems before they become emergencies.

**Applications**: Wearable ECG, arrhythmia detection, cardiac event monitoring, preventive care

---

### Chapter 2: Assessing Brain Health
**Detecting Problems Before Symptoms Show**

Your brain's electrical activity changes when something's wrong. Sometimes months or years before obvious symptoms appear. This chapter explores neurological assessment: concussion monitoring for athletes, early detection of cognitive decline, and sleep disorder diagnosis. Follow David's story as doctors use EEG to catch early signs of dementia.

**Applications**: Concussion protocols, dementia screening, seizure monitoring, brain health tracking

---

### Chapter 3: Understanding Sleep
**What Happens When You Close Your Eyes**

You spend a third of your life asleep, but most people don't know what's really happening. This chapter reveals how sleep tracking works, from consumer devices to clinical sleep studies. Learn the difference between light, deep, and REM sleep. Discover how sleep apnea shows up in data. Follow Marcus as he improves his sleep quality using biosignal feedback.

**Applications**: Sleep stage classification, apnea detection, sleep quality metrics, insomnia assessment

---

### Chapter 4: Stress and Emotion
**Measuring What You Feel**

Stress isn't just in your head - it shows up clearly in your biosignals. Heart rate variability, skin conductance, and breathing patterns all change with your emotional state. This chapter explores stress measurement, biofeedback training, and mental health applications. Learn how Jessica uses biofeedback to manage anxiety, and understand the ethical considerations of emotion recognition.

**Applications**: Stress assessment, biofeedback therapy, meditation training, mental health monitoring

---

### Chapter 5: Sports Performance
**The Data Behind Athletic Excellence**

Professional athletes use biosignals to train smarter, not just harder. This chapter takes you inside elite sports monitoring: measuring fatigue, optimizing recovery, preventing overtraining. Follow Olympic cyclist Tom Chen as he uses heart rate variability and lactate threshold data to optimize his training and win gold.

**Applications**: Training optimization, fatigue detection, recovery tracking, performance prediction

---

### Chapter 6: Healthy Aging
**Independence Through Monitoring**

As we age, small changes can indicate big problems. Fall risk increases. Activity patterns shift. Cognitive function changes. This chapter shows how continuous monitoring supports healthy aging and independence. Meet the Johnson family, who uses motion sensors and wearables to help Grandma live safely at home while giving everyone peace of mind.

**Applications**: Fall detection, activity monitoring, medication adherence, remote family support

---

### Chapter 7: Research and Discovery
**Contributing to Scientific Knowledge**

Every major breakthrough in biosignal analysis came from research. This chapter shows how scientists design studies, collect data, and make discoveries. Learn the basics of research design, data quality, and ethical practices. Understand how your data might contribute to science, and see the journey from hypothesis to published findings.

**Applications**: Study design, data collection, statistical analysis, publication process

---

### Chapter 8: Building Your Own Application
**Your Roadmap to Creating**

Ready to build something? This chapter is your practical guide. Learn how to choose sensors, set up data pipelines, and design simple applications. Follow a step-by-step example building a basic heart rate monitor app. Get resources for going deeper. This is where you transform from learner to creator.

**Applications**: Sensor selection, data acquisition, signal processing, app architecture

## Important Notes

### Privacy and Ethics

Throughout this book, we'll discuss real applications of biosignal monitoring. This technology is powerful, which means it raises important ethical questions:

- **Consent**: When should monitoring require explicit permission?
- **Privacy**: Who owns your biosignal data?
- **Bias**: How do we ensure technology works fairly for everyone?
- **Access**: Who gets these life-saving technologies?

We'll address these questions in every chapter. Technology is never neutral - how we use it matters.

### Medical Disclaimer

This book is educational, not medical advice. The examples show real applications, but:

- Always consult qualified healthcare providers for medical decisions
- Consumer devices are not substitutes for professional medical care
- If you have concerning symptoms, see a doctor - don't self-diagnose
- Research applications may not yet be validated for clinical use

Learn from these examples, but make health decisions with professional guidance.
